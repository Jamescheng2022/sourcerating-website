# BYH Thailand Digital System — Private Proposal Draft

Status: internal draft. Do not send until the private Preview is visually approved and the correct BYH recipient is verified.

## One-sentence proposal

Build BYH Thailand a Thai-first international website and a private multilingual employee AI workspace, then operate the website, knowledge, content and technical maintenance as one annual service.

## Why BYH now

- Phase I opened in October 2025 and Phase II is underway.
- The Thailand factory needs a local identity while remaining visibly connected to Wuxi BEST.
- Public information exists across parent filings, AMATA and the EPC announcement, but buyers and Thai employees do not have one controlled local entry point.
- A new factory can establish its digital operating standard before documents, channels and ownership become fragmented.

## What the private demonstration shows

1. Thai-first international website with separate Thai, English and Chinese URLs.
2. Thailand factory narrative connected to source evidence.
3. Structured engineering RFQ rather than a generic contact form.
4. Public assistant that refuses unsupported promises and cites sources.
5. BYH Works employee assistant for Thai, Chinese and English documents.
6. Approval, content and maintenance operations console.

Model/API, VPS, advanced SSO, third-party software, professional photography, video production and human technical translation are itemized separately.

## Demonstration script

1. Open the Thai website and show that the local plant is the primary subject, not a translated parent-company page.
2. Switch to English and Chinese URLs to show the regional buyer path.
3. Ask the public assistant about Phase II; point to the sources and the planned-capacity warning.
4. Open the RFQ flow and explain the engineering inputs.
5. Open BYH Works; ask a Thai question about a Chinese or English document.
6. Show the human-review warning for unapproved content.
7. Open Digital Operations to show annual maintenance, approvals and content planning.
8. End with the asset and document request, not a request to approve every visual detail in the first meeting.
