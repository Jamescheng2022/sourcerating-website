## Why technical illustration here?

A high-resolution photorealistic render of an ASTM tunnel, SBI apparatus or an actual test certificate risks suggesting a tested configuration or reproducing details we cannot independently verify. These figures instead distinguish (1) the two kinds of evidence, (2) construction and installation details that may change the field of application, and (3) the document-to-product-to-end-use identity chain. Figure 2 is explicitly symbolic and not a buildable assembly detail or dimensioned section. This is a topic-specific engineering-accuracy exception to the site's preferred industrial photography style, **subject to the Lead's independent IMAGE_EDITORIAL_PASS**.
